## Summary

The two Microsoft repositories separate the implementation and the public learning surface: microsoft/aspire supplies the product source and microsoft/aspire.dev supplies the official documentation, marketing, and integration catalog.

Both repositories make source available under MIT licenses, but an MIT license on a repository does not by itself confirm reuse rights for every screenshot or contributed media asset.

## Gaps and conflicts

No repository-owned screenshot assets were downloaded or published. The active host blocked image retrieval, and no separate asset-specific license was found for the official screenshots.
